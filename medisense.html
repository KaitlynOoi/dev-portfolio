Project — Embedded Systems · Healthcare

Medisense

A two-board Arduino BLE health monitoring system. The patient-side board collects vitals — SpO₂, heart rate, temperature, humidity, and movement — and transmits them wirelessly to a caregiver-side board via Bluetooth Low Energy. Patients can also send requests (water, restroom, help) via a resistor-ladder button array.

Arduino C++ BLE MAX30102 VL53L0X HTS221 Embedded Healthcare

System Architecture

Patient board

Broadcasts vitals over BLE as a peripheral device named PatientMonitor.

  • MAX30102 pulse oximeter — SpO₂ & heart rate
  • HTS221 sensor — temperature & humidity
  • VL53L0X ToF — movement classification (QUIET / ACTIVE / SPORADIC)
  • Resistor-ladder buttons — patient requests
  • RGB LED status indicator

Caregiver board

Scans for the patient peripheral, connects, and reads all BLE characteristics every 200 ms.

  • Decodes temperature & humidity with comfort thresholds
  • Displays movement state in human-readable form
  • Reads SpO₂ and heart rate with validity checks
  • Surfaces patient button requests in plain English
  • Auto-reconnects on disconnect
